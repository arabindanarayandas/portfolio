Put them on your phone

iPhone

Open a game in Safari, tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen".

Android

Open a game in Chrome and tap "Install", or use the browser menu and choose "Install app".

Every game keeps its own best score, and one daily streak and coin balance are shared across all of them. Everything is saved on your phone.
